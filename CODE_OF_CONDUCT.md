# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, identity, or personal characteristics.

Our goal is to make participation in AI Engineer Workbench a positive experience for all contributors.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and professional.
- Welcoming constructive feedback.
- Helping others learn and grow.
- Respecting different viewpoints.
- Focusing on technical discussions.
- Using inclusive language.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples include:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Offensive or inappropriate language.
- Trolling or insulting contributors.
- Publishing private information.
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe environment.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that violate these guidelines.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from the project.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Documentation
- Community interactions related to AI Engineer Workbench

---

## Contact

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open a private GitHub issue or contact the project maintainer.

---

Thank you for helping make AI Engineer Workbench a welcoming and professional open-source project.
